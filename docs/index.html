Speech drives everything

The voice is the clock.
Every word is a trigger.

Your voice, your way

Neural TTS narration — piper, XTTS, Qwen, or cloned chatterbox voices — synthesized on your machine. One narrator, a full panel, or no voice at all. Give each speaker a color; the captions follow.

Karaoke captions

Every word lights up exactly as it's spoken, in the speaker's color. No manual timing, ever.

Cue-timed reveals

data-cue="2" keeps an element hidden until turn 2 is spoken. Visuals land on the beat.

Any URL becomes source material

Product sites, articles, papers, docs, repos — narova extracts the facts and the visual evidence, then scripts from them.

Runs on your machine.

ffmpeg and neural TTS run locally. Some models, stock assets, and HyperFrames fetch over the network on first use.

Never the same video twice

Palette, layout, pacing, and one signature move are derived from each brief — not a template re-skinned. A paper walkthrough doesn't look like a product reel, and neither looks like anyone else's.

From idea to mp4

Three moves. That's it.

01

Ask naturally

Tell your agent what you want to make. Add a URL, repo, document, script, or just an idea.

02

Narova takes the lead

It recommends the story, voice, look, pacing, and format — then builds the complete editable project.

03

Preview, direct, finish

Watch the preview, ask for changes in your own words, and approve the final local render.

Your first video starts
with one prompt.

Install the skill once. After that, use Narova the way it was designed: talk to your agent. Pick a starting point or say it your way.

pick an example · ask your agent · render locally 
# install once
$ npx skills add ammar-hasan/narova

# idea → video
you → Make a 45-second vertical explainer about why starting
small makes habits easier to keep. Make it warm and practical,
and show me a preview before rendering.

agent → I’ll use one warm narrator, a calm visual rhythm, and
simple step-by-step scenes. I’ll keep the language practical
and show you the vertical preview first.
