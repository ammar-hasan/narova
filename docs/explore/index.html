# Creative brief — Narova skill reel

Status: approved

## Intent

Explain Narova's prompt → voice → synchronized motion pipeline in one compact,
first-party product reel. The purple field, lime/cyan/pink signals, large type,
and diagram-like scene changes make each production stage visually distinct.

## Approved pilot

The rendered hook and speech-synchronization scenes establish the intended
typography, palette, cue behavior, and diagram language. The published video is
the reviewed result.

## Rejection criteria

Reject if narration and reveals visibly disagree, captions obscure the scene,
the five stages become visually indistinguishable, text clips at 1280×720, or
the final encode loses spoken audio.

# Creative brief — one-command Narova demo

Status: approved

## Intent

Show the smallest honest Narova activation: one locally synthesized narrator,
two portable scenes, and restrained geometric motion that renders consistently
without browser-only effects.

## Approved pilot

The complete two-scene build is the pilot. Its centered play mark and staggered
dots demonstrate the portable visual vocabulary while keeping attention on the
editable command and project file.
